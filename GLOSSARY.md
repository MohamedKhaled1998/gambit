# Two-Faced

A social strategy party game for a friend group, set at a masquerade: guests trade favours, talk privately and betray each other between skill matches, and are unmasked round by round until two remain for the Last Dance.

"Gambit" is the repo's codename only; the game is Two-Faced.

## People

**Guest**:
Someone playing a game of Two-Faced; the word the game itself uses for its players.
_Avoid_: Contestant, participant (in game text, "player" too)

**Host**:
The guest who created the game; they run the game itself (starting it, helping a guest back to their seat) but get no say in play, and keep the role if unmasked.
_Avoid_: Admin, owner, organiser, game master

**In-person guest**:
A guest who has picked another guest they are playing with in person; their phone stays silent apart from quiet cues and shows captions, while that guest's phone is their Speaker.
_Avoid_: Same-room player, local player

**Speaker**:
The phone of a guest whom In-person guests have picked; it plays the narration, the music and the public cues aloud for them, and never a private cue, not even its own guest's.
_Avoid_: Room speaker, host phone, main phone

**Unmasked guest**:
A guest who has been unmasked; they stay until the game ends, watching from the Balcony, playing along with nothing at stake, and sitting on the Jury.
_Avoid_: Eliminated player, ghost, spectator

**Balcony**:
The chat every unmasked guest shares and no remaining guest can read.
_Avoid_: Graveyard, dead chat, audience

**Jury**:
The unmasked guests, voting together in the Last Dance for which finalist wins.
_Avoid_: Council, audience

**Prediction**:
An unmasked guest's pick of who a Cellar game unmasks or who wins the Last Dance's match (not the Last Dance itself, which jurors vote on); it changes nothing in the game and is revealed to everyone only at the end.
_Avoid_: Bet, wager

**Finalist**:
One of the two guests left for the Last Dance. Game text calls them "the last two".
_Avoid_: Contender, champion

**Last mask**:
The end-of-game title for the finalist who wins the Last Dance, the one guest never unmasked.
_Avoid_: Champion, winner (as the title)

**Oracle**:
The end-of-game title for the unmasked guest whose predictions scored best.

## Formats

**Quick game**:
A game of three rounds and the Last Dance, for 4-10 guests.

**Full season**:
A game of five to seven rounds and the Last Dance, for 6-10 guests, resumable across days.
_Avoid_: Long game, campaign

## Joining a game

**Foyer**:
Where guests gather before a game starts, having joined with the Invitation code; the Host picks the format there, and starting the game closes its doors.
_Avoid_: Lobby, waiting room

**Invitation code**:
The short code a guest enters to join a game in its Foyer.
_Avoid_: Room code, game code, join code

## Round structure

**Round**:
One pass through the Mingle, the Dance, being sent down, the Cellar game and unmasking. The Last Dance is not a round.
_Avoid_: Evening, episode

**Mingle**:
The opening phase of a round, when guests make deals and talk privately before the Dance.
_Avoid_: Free time, lobby, break

**Dance**:
The skill match every remaining guest plays in a round.
_Avoid_: Main match, main event, main game

**Sent down**:
What happens after the Dance to the guests holding the fewest favours: they go to the Cellar.
_Avoid_: Sentencing, jailed, imprisoned

**Cellar**:
The group of guests sent down in a round; it always holds more guests than the round unmasks.
_Avoid_: Prison, jail, danger zone

**Free guest**:
A remaining guest who was not sent down this round.
_Avoid_: Safe guest, survivor

**Cellar game**:
The match only guests in the Cellar play, drawn at random from the matches that can serve as one and announced when they are sent down; they all play at once and the lowest-ranked are unmasked. In a round that unmasks nobody it is played for practice, with nothing at stake. Free guests watch it live but can't talk to anyone in the Cellar while it runs.
_Avoid_: Prison game, prison match

**Cellar talk**:
The chat that opens for exactly the guests in the Cellar while their Cellar game runs; free guests can't read it, and it closes at unmasking.
_Avoid_: Prison chat, cell chat

**Unmasking**:
A guest losing their place in the game for good, normally by ranking lowest in a round's Cellar game, or by leaving; that guest is unmasked.
_Avoid_: Elimination, knockout, death

**Leaving**:
A guest choosing to unmask themselves early, because they have to go; game text says they "left the party". Only the guest can do it: an absent guest is never declared gone.
_Avoid_: Dropout, quitting, forfeit, removal

**Unmasking schedule**:
How many guests each remaining round unmasks so that exactly two reach the Last Dance; hidden from guests, and recomputed after every round.
_Avoid_: Elimination schedule

**Unmasking vote**:
A secret ballot, revealed with names, that decides whether a round unmasks its lower count or one more; it comes unannounced, at the start of a round or during its Dance, and never in the last round.
_Avoid_: Elimination vote, poll, referendum

**Mercy**:
The unmasking vote option for the lower count; it delays an unmasking to a later round rather than cancelling it.

**Greed**:
The unmasking vote option for one more unmasking, which pays the voter a personal reward whatever the outcome.
_Avoid_: Betrayal (betrayal is any broken deal, not a vote option)

**Warm-up round**:
A round that unmasks nobody, at the start of a game or after an unmasking vote; it is played in full, and only its Dance's payout is at stake.
_Avoid_: Zero-elimination round, amnesty round, practice round

**Last Dance**:
The end of the game, after the last round: the two finalists play one match head to head, address the Jury at the Table, and the Jury's vote, weighed against that match, decides the winner. No Mingle comes before it.
_Avoid_: Finale, final

## Favours, deals and talk

**Favour**:
The game's single currency, and what guests are sent down by; every guest starts with ten. Holding none never unmasks a guest.
_Avoid_: Token, chip, coin, gem

**Deal**:
An agreement between guests; the game never enforces it, so any part not paid on the spot is only a promise.
_Avoid_: Contract, pact

**Betrayal**:
Breaking a deal.

**Gift**:
Whole favours one guest hands another during the Mingle; instant, final, and known only to the two of them.
_Avoid_: Trade, transfer, payment

**Salon**:
The shared home space of a game, where guests gather between phases and see the standings, the Table and who is in which Room.
_Avoid_: Hub, lobby, lounge, house

**Table**:
The chat in the Salon that every remaining guest shares; during the Last Dance it opens to the whole party, unmasked guests included.
_Avoid_: Lobby, global chat

**Room**:
A private chat some guests share, leading off the Salon; who is in it is public, what is said is not.
_Avoid_: DM, channel, group

## Matches

**Match type**:
A kind of play with its own rules, such as a race to answer, a sealed bid or a memory grid; every match is one match type with chosen settings.
_Avoid_: Game mode, mechanic

**Match**:
A match type with its settings (name, rules text, guest range, length, difficulty, problem bank), usable as a Dance, a Cellar game or both.
_Avoid_: Game, challenge, minigame

**Match library**:
Every match the game can draw a Dance or a Cellar game from; a match is drawn again in one game only when no unused match fits.
_Avoid_: Catalogue, pool, playlist

**Encore**:
A match drawn a second time in the same game, with fresh problems where it has a problem bank and no Prelude.
_Avoid_: Repeat, rerun, replay

**Problem bank**:
The problems a match draws from: either a written list or a generator that makes problems to given settings.
_Avoid_: Question pool, deck

**Problem**:
One puzzle, question or grid a guest solves in a step; every guest in a match gets the same problems in the same order.
_Avoid_: Question, puzzle (as the general term)

**Step**:
One unit of a match, such as a problem, a bidding turn or a reveal; a match is a sequence of steps, and it can pause only between them.
_Avoid_: Turn (as the general term), stage, level

**Ranking**:
The strict order of a match's guests, with no ties, that every match ends with; each match breaks ties by its own rules. A Dance's ranking sets its payout and breaks ties in favours when guests are sent down; a Cellar game's ranking decides who is unmasked.
_Avoid_: Leaderboard, result, standings

**Points**:
The score a guest builds within one match, starting at zero each match, that orders the ranking; favours can buy points.
_Avoid_: Score (as a lasting total)

**Payout table**:
The favours a Dance pays or takes from each place in its ranking, set by how many guests remain; a default table applies unless the match sets its own.
_Avoid_: Prize table, rewards

## Teaching the rules

**Narrator**:
The unseen, unnamed voice of the house; it sets the scene, explains each part of the game the first time it comes, and only announces it after that. It never speaks a guest's name.
_Avoid_: Announcer, MC, host, game master

**Overture**:
The short narrated scene-setter that opens a game as the Foyer's doors close, before the first round.
_Avoid_: Intro, tutorial, onboarding

**Prelude**:
The narrated play-through of a match's worked example on its own screens, shown just before the match starts; a guest can skip it, and the match starts early once everyone in it has.
_Avoid_: Explainer video, tutorial, how-to

**Rules card**:
A match's rules summary and worked example as text, shown to a guest who skips its Prelude and always available while the match is announced or running.
_Avoid_: Help page, rulebook
